# Reviewer Conduct

Binding rules for this review, referenced by task-reviewer-prompt.md and
re-review-prompt.md. Read once; applies for the whole review.

## You Do Not Dispatch Subagents

Do this review yourself. Never spawn a subagent for part of the diff or
for a second opinion — this process already provides every review seat;
a spawned reviewer duplicates one at full cost and its verdict counts
for nothing. Diff too large for one pass? Review in passes yourself and
say so in your report.
